About me

I am a Data & Analytics Engineer specialized in building robust, end-to-end data processing pipelines and analytical storage. With an academic background in Computer Science and a Master's degree in Big Data Analytics from USTHB, I focus on the structural integrity of data systems, ensuring data moves efficiently, stays reliable under load, and serves immediate operational needs.

My engineering philosophy centers on context-driven design. I treat every data challenge as unique: I analyze business workflows, query patterns, and scale requirements first. This proactive approach ensures the architecture, tools, and data models selected fit the exact problem at hand, delivering long-term reliability and measurable value.

  • Pragmatic & Context-Driven Architecture.Analyzing throughput, scale, and business needs before choosing tools—ensuring the stack fits the problem.
  • Proactive Reliability & Observability.Embedding schema validation, health monitoring, and automated alerts into code to catch failures before end-users do.
  • Deterministic & Scalable Environments.Leveraging containerized infrastructure so pipelines execute identically across local, staging, and production setups.
